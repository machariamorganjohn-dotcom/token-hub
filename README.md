## 📱 Mobile Testing (Physical Phone)
1.  **Connect**: Ensure your phone is on the same Wi-Fi as this computer.
2.  **Server IP**: The app is configured to talk to your computer at `192.168.8.159`.
3.  **Install**: Once the build finishes, find your APK here:
    `build/app/outputs/flutter-apk/app-arm64-v8a-release.apk`
4.  **Run Backend**: Ensure the backend server is running (`node server.js`) before opening the app on your phone.
